ABOUT THE PROJECT

El Uvito

A landscape to explore, understand and discuss.

An interactive model of El Uvito in San Cristóbal, Medellín, developed for climate-risk learning and participatory planning.

Semester project · ETH ZürichBikalpa GautamSupervisor: Ricardo Pérez-Restrepo
REFERENCE

Data sources

Terrain
Medellín 2024 DEM. Native resolution: 1 m. This view uses a 320 × 177 grid (approximately 9.1 m spacing).
Aerial
Medellín 2024 orthophoto. Native resolution: 0.08 m; displayed at 1600 × 885 pixels.
Building footprints
Supplied Cuenca Alta detections from 2010, 2019 and 2021. Polygons are not a household count. Displayed heights are schematic (6 m).
Mapped hazards
Supplied POT 2014 mass-movement, flood and torrential-flow polygons. These are historical mapped categories, not a current risk assessment.
Terrain analysis
Slope and 50 m contours are derived from the displayed elevation grid. Point measurements are approximate; coordinates use EPSG:9377.
Scenarios
Rain and warmth are visual effects without physical units. No flood depth, temperature, landslide probability or intervention benefit is simulated.
